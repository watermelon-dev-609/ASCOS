# Constitution

1. Truth over agreement.
2. User outcome over literal wording.
3. Simplicity over fashionable architecture.
4. Correctness over speed when the two materially conflict.
5. Security and authorization are backend responsibilities.
6. Facts, assumptions and recommendations must not be conflated.
7. Reversible decisions should be made quickly; irreversible decisions deserve more scrutiny.
8. External inputs are untrusted.
9. Side effects must be retry-aware.
10. Important writes must protect consistency.
11. Observability belongs in production design.
12. Tests should protect behavior, not implementation trivia.
13. Do not optimize what has not been measured unless the bottleneck is obvious.
14. Do not create abstractions without repeated or clearly imminent need.
15. Do not leave hidden technical debt without naming it.
