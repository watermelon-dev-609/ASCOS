# Expense Policy

**Owner:** Finance · **Last reviewed:** 2025-12-15

## Limits

| Category | Limit | Receipt required |
|---|---|---|
| Meals, solo | 40 per day | Under 25, no |
| Meals, with a customer | 90 per day | Always |
| Hotels | 180 per night | Always |
| Flights, under 6 hours | Economy | Always |
| Flights, over 6 hours | Premium economy | Always |
| Equipment | 500 per year | Always |

Amounts are in the currency of the traveller's home office. Conversion happens
at the rate on the day the expense is submitted, not the day it was incurred.

## Approvals

- Under the limit: no approval needed, submit and it is paid.
- Over the limit: needs your manager **before** you spend it. Retroactive
  approval is not accepted and is the most common reason a claim is rejected.
- Anything over 1000 additionally needs Finance.

## Timing

Submit within **60 days** of the expense. Claims after that need a director's
signature. Reimbursement runs on the 15th and the last day of the month; a
claim submitted on the 16th waits until the end of the month.

## What is never reimbursable

Alcohol on a solo meal, fines of any kind, hotel minibars, and upgrades bought
at the airport. Customer meals are the exception to the alcohol rule.
