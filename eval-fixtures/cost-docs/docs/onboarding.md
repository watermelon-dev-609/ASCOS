# Onboarding

**Owner:** People Ops · **Last reviewed:** 2025-11-04

## Your first week

1. Accounts are created by IT before your start date. If you have no account on
   day one, ask in `#it-helpdesk` — do not ask your manager, they cannot create
   accounts.
2. Read `deployment.md` and `incident-runbook.md` before you take any on-call
   shift. Both are required regardless of role.
3. Pick up a starter ticket from the `#eng-backlog` channel. Starter tickets are
   tagged `good-first-issue`.

## Who owns what

| Area | Team | Escalation |
|---|---|---|
| Deployments and environments | Platform | `#platform-oncall` |
| Customer data questions | Data | `#data-help` |
| Expenses and reimbursements | Finance | `#finance` |
| Anything about the public API | API guild | `#api-guild` |

## Access levels

There are three levels. Most engineers need **level 2**. Level 3 gives access
to production databases and requires a ticket approved by the Data team lead.
Level 3 is reviewed every 90 days and expires automatically if not renewed.

> Note: this section was written when there were four access levels. Level 1
> (read-only) was folded into level 2 in March. Older documents still mention
> it; treat those mentions as stale.
